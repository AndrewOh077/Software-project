How it heals

  1. 1

    Notice

    The controller checks every server every second. Dead ones leave traffic.

  2. 2

    Repair

    It restarts them and brings traffic back slowly: 10, 25, 50, 100%.

  3. 3

    Warn early

    The AI spots servers that still say “fine” but fail or slow down.
