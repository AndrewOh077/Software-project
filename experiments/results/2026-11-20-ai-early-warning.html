A crash is the health check’s job

A killed server is caught in 0.1 s and gets no traffic after that, so the AI has nothing to judge. That is fine.

Two in a row stops false alarms

Z-score false alarms across all runs: 1 when one odd sample is enough, 0 when it needs two in a row. The cost is one more sample: 5 s slower.

The forest adds nothing yet

With only two numbers per server, Isolation Forest finds the same faults as a z-score. It should help once we add more numbers.
